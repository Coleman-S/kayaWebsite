About Venice

Venice is a city in the northeastern part of Italy. The city is built on over 100 small islands that work together with canals and open water as the mode of transportation. There are no roads! This is a historic city that holds a lot of Renaissance and Gothic architecture and history. On the island city there are about 51,000 residents. However, Venice experiences roughly 30 million visitors each year.

As Venice was once the capital of the Venetian Republic, held maritime power throughout the Middle Ages and Renaissance, and was a point of commerce, it was a well positioned city.

For my abroad experience, I spent two weeks exploring the island city of Venice, interviewing locals and learning more about true Venetian culture and cuisine. Here, you can find the journalistic article and photo essay I created while there.
