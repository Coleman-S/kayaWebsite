About This Portfolio

My Journey Through European Cities

Welcome to my city exploration portfolio! I'm a student with a passion for history, architecture, and cultural exploration. This portfolio showcases my journey through four magnificent European cities: Venice, Florence, Rome, and Valletta.

Each city page features an in-depth article exploring the history, culture, and significance of the city, as well as a photo essay documenting my visual experiences. The articles and photo essays are available as downloadable PDFs for easy reading and sharing.

This project was created as part of my studies to document and share my experiences exploring these historic European cities. Through this portfolio, I hope to convey not just the visual beauty of these places, but also their historical significance and cultural impact.

Feel free to explore each city page, read the articles, and enjoy the photo essays. If you have any questions or would like to discuss any aspect of these cities, please don't hesitate to contact me.

About the Website

This website was built using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and designed to be responsive, ensuring a good viewing experience across different devices.

The PDF articles and photo essays are original works created to showcase my research and photography from each city.
