My Journey Through European Cities

I have a bachelor's degree in Mass Communication with a concentration on Multi-Platform and a Global Studies minor concentrated on Sustainable Development. I have spent some time in the spring of 2025 learning more about storytelling through art and journalism helps me remember that everyone everywhere has a story to tell. A painting, a sculpture, they both have stories and histories to tell and to share. I am learning how to tell those stories and when to tell those stories in order to do the most good. I love being able to tell a story in a narrative that flows

I have written for The Pilot newspaper since the fall of 2022 working as a staff writer and editor. I have loved thinking about community and change around campus.
